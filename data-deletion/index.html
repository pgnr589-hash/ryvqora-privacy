Instructions for disconnecting accounts and requesting deletion

You can remove locally stored RYVQORA information and revoke the app's access to connected services by following the steps below.

1. Disconnect a connected service

  1. Open RYVQORA AI and use its account or integrations settings to disconnect the service, if that control is available in your version.
  2. Visit the connected service's own security, app permissions, or business integrations settings.
  3. Find RYVQORA AI (or the app name shown during authorization) and remove/revoke its access.

2. Remove locally stored app information

  1. Close RYVQORA AI.
  2. Use the app's built-in clear-data/reset option if your version provides one.
  3. If no reset option exists, use your operating system's app settings or the app's documented data-folder instructions to remove RYVQORA's local data. Back up anything you want to keep first.
  4. Restart the app. You may need to authorize integrations again if you reconnect them.

Uninstalling the app alone may not remove every local data folder. The exact location depends on the operating system and app build.

3. Request help with deletion

Email pgnr589@gmail.com with the subject “RYVQORA AI data deletion request”. Describe the connected service and the information you want help identifying. Do not email passwords, access tokens, API keys, or other login credentials.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant app installation or request, but you should not send more personal information than necessary. Information already held by Meta, another social platform, or an AI provider must generally be deleted through that provider's own controls and is subject to its policies.

4. Important note for platform developers

This is a public information page with user-facing deletion instructions. A static GitHub Pages website does not provide a server-side Data Deletion Callback endpoint and cannot receive or process automated POST callbacks from Meta. If Meta's developer dashboard specifically requires a Data Deletion Callback URL, you must implement and host a secure HTTPS backend endpoint that follows Meta's current callback requirements. Do not enter this page URL as a callback endpoint unless Meta explicitly accepts an instructions URL for the setting you are configuring.
