Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RYVQORA AI (“RYVQORA”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the application and connect third-party services.

1. Information the app may handle

Depending on the features you choose to use, RYVQORA may handle:

  • Account and profile information made available by a connected service, such as a public name, profile identifier, or page/account details.
  • Authentication information, such as access tokens or authorization credentials needed to connect an account.
  • Content and activity you choose to manage, such as drafts, captions, media references, scheduled-post details, comments, or messages.
  • Information you enter into the app, including prompts, settings, and configuration details.
  • Basic diagnostic information needed to troubleshoot app errors, if diagnostics are enabled.

The exact information depends on the integrations, permissions, and features enabled in your installed version.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, connect to services you authorize, prepare or manage content, respond to your instructions, maintain app settings, and troubleshoot problems.

3. Local storage and third-party services

RYVQORA is currently designed to store connected-service authentication tokens locally on the device where the app is installed. Keep your device account protected and do not share app data folders or diagnostic files that may contain sensitive information.

When you use an integration or AI provider, the app may send the information needed for that feature to the provider you selected. For example, a social platform may receive requests to read account information or perform an action you authorized; an AI provider may receive prompts or content you submit for processing. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Do not assume that every type of app data is stored only on your device. Network requests to third-party services are necessary for connected features. Review the permissions shown by each provider before authorizing access.

4. Sharing

We do not describe third-party API processing as “no sharing.” When you use a connected platform or AI provider, information is transmitted to that provider as needed to carry out your request. RYVQORA does not intend to sell personal information. Do not use the app to submit information you do not have permission to share.

5. Retention and deletion

Authentication tokens and other locally stored app information may remain on your device until you disconnect an account, clear the app's local data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling may not remove every app data folder on every operating system. Information already sent to a third-party provider is subject to that provider's retention and deletion practices.

For instructions on disconnecting accounts and requesting deletion, visit our Data Deletion page.

6. Security

We aim to limit access to locally stored credentials and recommend that users keep the app updated and protect their device. No software or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Never publish access tokens, API keys, client secrets, or exported app data.

7. Children's privacy

RYVQORA is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or third-party platform age requirements. Users should follow the minimum-age requirements and terms of every service they connect.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, integrations, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are published. Check this page periodically.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact pgnr589@gmail.com.
